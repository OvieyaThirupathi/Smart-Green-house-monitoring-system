

**🌱 SMART GREENHOUSE AUTOMATION SYSTEM**

**1\. Aim**

To design and develop a smart greenhouse system that automatically monitors environmental conditions and controls temperature, soil moisture, and rain protection using sensors and actuators.

**2\. Problem Statement**

In a greenhouse, plants require suitable temperature, moisture, and protection from excessive rain for healthy growth. Manual monitoring and control of these conditions can require continuous attention and may lead to improper watering or temperature control.

**3\. Proposed Solution**

The proposed Smart Greenhouse uses sensors to continuously monitor the conditions inside and around the greenhouse.

When the temperature becomes high, the fan automatically turns ON.

When the soil becomes dry, the water pump automatically supplies water.

When excessive rain is detected, the greenhouse roof/cover automatically closes to protect the plants.

Thus, the system provides automatic environmental control with minimum manual intervention.

**5\. Circuit Working**

The sensors continuously monitor the conditions of the greenhouse.

The temperature and humidity sensor measures the surrounding temperature. When the temperature crosses the predefined limit, Arduino activates the fan automatically.

The soil moisture sensor checks the moisture level of the soil. When the soil becomes dry, Arduino turns ON the water pump and supplies water to the plants.

The rain drop sensor detects excessive rain. When rain is detected above the set condition, Arduino controls the servo motor to close the greenhouse roof/cover automatically.

**6\. Procedure**

1\. Connect the temperature and humidity sensor to the Arduino.

2\. Connect the soil moisture sensor to the Arduino.

3\. Connect the rain drop sensor to the Arduino.

4\. Connect the fan and water pump through suitable switching/relay circuits.

5\. Connect the servo motor to the greenhouse roof mechanism.

6\. Upload the Arduino program.

7\. Set the required temperature and soil moisture limits.

8\. Test the system by increasing the temperature.

9\. Test the soil moisture sensor using dry soil.

10\. Test the rain sensor by applying water to its sensing surface.

11\. Observe the automatic operation of the fan, pump, and roof.
